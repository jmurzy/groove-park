# How to Play Heavenly

## Approach

The first playable zone is the approach. It is a bounded area of snow where you
build speed and choose a line. Reaching its edge ends the run for now.

- Hold **Right** to commit downhill and let gravity build speed.
- Press **Up** or **Down** to carve across the slope.
- Hold **A** to tuck. It reduces drag but makes turning slower.
- Hold **Y** for a stronger carve that costs speed.
- Hold **B** to check speed.

Right is a held downhill commitment: release it and the rider carves to a stop.
The joystick still sets the rider's intended direction, so Up and Down guide
the carve across the slope.

## Current Scope

Only approach movement is active. Compression, takeoff, flight, tricks,
landing, and scoring will be added one zone at a time after the approach feels
right.
