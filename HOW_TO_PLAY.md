# Groove Park at Heavenly: How to Play

Groove Park is a solo arcade ski and snowboard game. Build speed on the approach, choose a route, charge and release a pop at the lip, add a trick in the air, then prepare early enough to land it. A round lasts up to three jumps; one crash ends the round.

## Start A Run

1. At the title screen, choose **START GAME**, or press **START**.
2. Pick **SNOWBOARDER** or **SKIER** with left/right, then press **A** or **START**. Both riders use the same game mechanics and scoring.
3. You begin on the center route. Press **W/up** or **S/down** during the approach to move to a neighboring authored route.

The upper and center routes launch into the air. The lower route continues directly into a ground runout, so it is useful for carrying a run safely but does not create a flight trick opportunity.

On the Polycade Sente cabinet, either identical player station controls the one active rider. The white **EXIT** button is a system control: hold it to quit from the cabinet. The blue **X** button is the in-game action button, not Exit.

## The Approach

Your goal is to arrive at the lip with enough speed and a fully timed pop.

- **Hold right/D to build speed.** Gravity also contributes as the route falls away. Releasing right reduces your drive and adds carve drag.
- **Hold A/J to tuck.** Tucking reduces air resistance and helps preserve speed, but makes steering less responsive.
- **Use left or B to slow down.** B applies braking and makes steering more responsive. Use it to avoid overshooting your intended route or to control a risky approach.
- **Use W/S deliberately.** Each press changes to the neighboring route. The route shift is for choosing a line, not for weaving continuously across the screen.
- **Hold X/L to compress.** Compression charges while you approach the lip. Release it as close to the lip as possible for the best pop. Releasing too early weakens the takeoff; holding through the lip still launches you, but with an automatic, reduced-quality release.

### Approach Tips

- Start moving immediately: an approach that loses too much momentum ends as a low-momentum run with no useful score.
- Tuck on a clean, straight section to build speed, then come out of it before a route correction if you need sharper steering.
- Do not brake all the way to the lip. Speed contributes points, gives more airtime, and can be necessary to complete the rotations demanded by a fast takeoff.
- Charge X late. A full charge released at the lip produces the highest pop score and the biggest flight arc.

## In The Air

After leaving an upper or center lip, the controls become trick controls.

### Grabs

- **Hold A/J** for a standard grab.
- **Hold B/K** for a tweak grab. A valid tweak earns the usual grab points plus a style bonus.
- A grab must be held long enough to count. Brief taps do not score.
- Release a grab before landing preparation. You cannot land while a grab is still active.

You cannot change a standard grab into a tweak, or vice versa, without releasing the current grab and starting another one.

### 360 Spins

Spins only work while a grab or tweak grab is active.

1. Hold **A** or **B** to begin a grab.
2. Tap **LT/Q** for a left spin. For snowboarders this is a **backside** spin; skiers see it as **left**.
3. Let the trigger return to neutral after the first 180 degrees.
4. Tap that same trigger again to complete the second 180 and score a 360.

Use **RT/E** instead for a right spin. For snowboarders this is **frontside**; skiers see it as **right**. You must re-arm the same trigger between the two presses. Letting go of the grab while you are partway through a rotation makes the rotation incomplete and causes a crash if you reach the landing that way.

The HUD's **ROTATION** readout shows your accumulated degrees. **SPINS** shows completed rotations against the number required by your takeoff speed. At very high speeds, you may be required to complete one or more full rotations to land safely.

### Landing

**Hold X/L on the way down to prepare for landing.** It is the most important input at the end of every trick.

- A clean landing requires landing prep, no active grab, and no unfinished rotation.
- The horizontal release line near the lip's height is your warning. If you still hold a grab when you descend through that line, release it promptly.
- Releasing a grab after that deadline produces a **SKETCHY** landing instead of a clean one. Sketchy landings keep the jump alive but cut its score in half.
- Reaching the snow without landing prep, while holding a grab, or with an incomplete spin is a **CRASH**.
- Missing the intended landing can become a **BAIL**. A bail finishes the run without a clean landing bonus.

### Air Tips

- Get the grab established early, then start the first 180 while there is enough air remaining for the re-press and landing prep.
- For a dependable score, use one long grab or tweak, release it before the warning line, and hold X through contact.
- Do not chase a 360 with a slow takeoff. Rotation speed scales with takeoff speed, so a low-speed spin may not finish before the landing.
- Do not overdo speed without planning the spin. Above the safe takeoff speed, the HUD's SPINS quota tells you how many full rotations you need.

## Scoring

Each completed jump is scored from these components:

| Component | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| Approach | Carry more speed to the lip. |
| Takeoff | Charge compression and release X close to the lip. |
| Airtime | Generate a larger, cleaner flight arc. |
| Rotation | Complete 360-degree rotations. Each full rotation scores points. |
| Grab | Hold a valid grab for longer; its score caps after a short duration. |
| Style bonus | Complete a valid tweak grab. |
| Landing multiplier | Clean = x1.0; sketchy = x0.5; bail or crash = x0.0. |

The in-run SCORE is a clean-landing preview added to your accumulated round score. The post-jump tally shows the final outcome, trick summary, every score component, landing multiplier, jump score, and running total.

## Round Flow And High Scores

- A normal round has **three jumps**.
- Press **START** or **A** during a jump tally to hurry the count-up and continue to the next jump.
- A crash starts a rescue sequence and ends the round. After the minimum rescue display, press **START** or **A** to continue; it also advances automatically.
- At the end of the round, a qualifying online score opens name entry. Use the stick or arrow keys to select letters, **A/J** to add one, **B/K** or Backspace to delete, **X/L** to skip, and **START/Enter** to submit.
- Names use up to 12 uppercase letters and numbers. Name entry automatically skips an empty name or submits a non-empty name after its timeout.
- The results screen lists each jump, the final total, and leaderboard status when available. Press **START/A** for a new round or **SELECT/B** to return to attract mode.

## Pause And Help

Press **START**, **SELECT**, **EXIT**, or **Escape** during gameplay to pause. The pause menu lets you keep playing, open this how-to-play guide, or abort the run. Aborting discards the current round score and returns to the title flow.

From the title screen, select **HOW TO PLAY** or press **Y**. On the guide, **SELECT** switches between approach and air cards. Press **Escape** (the UI-cancel action) to return to the menu.

## Quick First Clean Run

1. Hold right/D to start moving, then hold A/J to tuck once the line is straight.
2. Choose the center or upper route with W/S if needed.
3. Hold X/L late on the approach, then release it right at the lip.
4. In the air, hold A/J for a grab.
5. Release A/J before you descend to lip height.
6. Hold X/L for the landing and keep holding it through contact.

Once that is consistent, replace the standard grab with a B/K tweak for the style bonus, then add the two-tap LT or RT 360 gesture.
